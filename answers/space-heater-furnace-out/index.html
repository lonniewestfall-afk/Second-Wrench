Space Heaters When the Furnace Is Out

Last updated October 29, 2026.

If you smell gas or rotten eggs in the air, not just from the hot water, leave the house now and call the gas utility or 911 from outside. Don’t touch light switches or phones, or use lighters, matches, or anything that could spark or flame while you’re inside.

If a carbon monoxide alarm is sounding, leave first. A space heater does not fix that. Get everyone outside and call 911 from outside.

An electric space heater can warm a room while you wait for a repair. It does not make a damaged furnace all right to run. If the furnace smells like burning plastic, or a breaker keeps tripping, leave the furnace off.

Do not heat the room this way

Never heat with an oven, stove, grill, charcoal, or generator indoors. Those can fill a room with carbon monoxide. CPSC winter weather tips. Don’t cook on a charcoal grill in a garage, even with the door open. Don’t run a generator in a garage, basement, crawlspace, or porch. CPSC says to keep a portable generator outside, at least 20 feet from the house, with the exhaust pointed away from doors and windows.

If you use an electric space heater

  • Plug it straight into a wall outlet. Don’t use a power strip or an extension cord.
  • Don’t run the cord under a rug or carpet.
  • Keep all sides at least three feet from beds, clothes, curtains, papers, sofas, and anything else that can burn.
  • Put it on a stable, level surface where it won’t be knocked over.
  • Don’t leave it running unattended in a confined space. CPSC also says to be mindful of children and pets around it.
  • Keep it away from water. Don’t touch it if you are wet.
  • One heater per outlet. If the cord, plug, or outlet gets hot, unplug the heater and have a licensed electrician check the outlet before you use it again.
  • Follow the heater maker’s instructions. If the heater, cord, or plug is damaged, don’t use it.

The furnace stays closed

If you still need to shut the furnace off: turn the heat Off at the thermostat, then switch off the furnace’s service switch or its breaker (switch it Off only). Never open the panel cover. Don’t reset a breaker that trips again. Don’t run the heat again until a licensed HVAC professional has checked it. If you see smoke or the smell gets stronger, get everyone outside and call 911 from outside.

Don’t open the furnace, relight a pilot, or bypass a safety so it runs beside the space heater. If someone is at risk from the cold, get them somewhere warm. An apartment with no heat has a separate page.

Start the guided check and choose Furnace or air handler, then No heat: nothing seems to happen.
