Boiler maintenance checklist

Last updated October 29, 2026.

This is a look-only list for a hot-water or steam boiler, baseboard, or radiators. It does not open the boiler, turn a gas valve, or bleed a radiator or baseboard.

If you smell gas or rotten eggs in the air, leave the house now and call the gas utility or 911 from outside. Don’t touch light switches or phones until you are outside. A rotten-egg smell only from the hot water goes to the rotten-egg smell page.

If hot water or steam is spraying, or the relief pipe is discharging, stay back. Don’t touch a valve or try to plug the pipe. Call a licensed heating professional. For a serious burn, call 911.

Warranty note: Your warranty may require professional maintenance or product registration. Check your warranty and your owner’s manual before you do a task yourself. Some makers require service records or work by a licensed pro. When in doubt, ask your installer or the maker. Following a checklist here does not keep, protect, or extend a warranty.

Pick a time you’ll remember. Your manual sets how often.

Alarms and the thermostat

Test carbon monoxide and smoke alarms with their test buttons. If a CO alarm sounds for real, get everyone outside and call 911. On the thermostat, don’t take it off the wall. Set Heat and a setpoint a few degrees above the room temperature. Radiators and floors can take a while to warm up. Some homes have more than one thermostat, one per zone.

Around the boiler

  • Look for water on the floor, rust at fittings, or a drip from the relief pipe. Don’t lift, cap, or plug the relief valve.
  • If you can already read the gauge from where you stand, write the number down. If it sits on a relief setting or warning mark identified for your boiler, don’t touch it. Call a licensed heating professional.
  • On a steam boiler, if the sight glass shows no water, don’t add water. Call a pro.
  • Look at the boiler’s switch and any nearby service switch. Don’t flip a switch or a breaker. If one is off or a breaker looks tripped, leave it and call.
  • Write down a code on the display. Don’t clear it, and don’t open the jacket to find a chart.

Rooms

Move furniture, curtains, and covers off baseboards and radiators so air can move. Give the system time after you do. Gurgling, or a radiator that is warm at the bottom and cold at the top, needs a pro. This page does not include bleeding instructions for radiators or baseboard.

What stays with a pro

  • Burners, the gas valve, the pilot, and any oil-burner reset. Don’t press a red reset button.
  • Opening the boiler, adding water, or adjusting pressure.
  • Bleeding radiators or baseboard, and zone valves.
  • Electrical work inside a panel. Never open the breaker panel cover.

If the heat is already out, use boiler or radiators not heating and start the guided check. Choose Something else, then Boiler, radiators, baseboard, or radiant floors.
