Thermostat maintenance checklist

Last updated October 29, 2026.

These checks use the thermostat you already have on the wall. Don’t pull it off, and don’t loosen wires. If a step asks for an installer menu, a password, or a wiring choice, stop and call the installer or a licensed HVAC professional.

Warranty note: Your warranty may require professional maintenance or product registration. Check your warranty and your owner’s manual before you do a task yourself. Some makers require service records or work by a licensed pro. When in doubt, ask your installer or the maker. Following a checklist here does not keep, protect, or extend a warranty.

Pick a time you’ll remember. Your manual sets how often.

Batteries

If your model takes batteries and the manual shows a cover that opens by hand, put in fresh batteries of the type it names. If the cover doesn’t match the manual, or you would have to take the thermostat off the wall, stop. A blank screen has its own page: smart thermostat has no power.

Mode, fan, and schedule

  • Heat season: mode Heat, setpoint a few degrees above the room, fan Auto. Fan On runs the blower between cycles and can feel cold.
  • Cool season: mode Cool, setpoint below the room temperature.
  • A heat pump: leave Emergency Heat and Aux off unless you are waiting on a pro. They usually cost more to run. Don’t use a deep setback unless the thermostat is made for a heat pump. See why a heating bill runs high.
  • If the temperature changes on its own, check for a hold, a schedule, or an Eco or Away setting before you assume the equipment failed.

What not to change

Don’t change equipment type, wiring, cycle rate, or other installer settings to chase a problem. Don’t reset a breaker for the furnace or air conditioner from this page. If a breaker looks tripped, leave it and call a licensed professional. Never open the breaker panel cover.
