What Size Furnace Filter Do I Need?

Last updated October 29, 2026.

The size is printed on the filter’s frame, not guessed from the furnace brand. It is three numbers: length, width, and thickness. Buy that size. A thicker filter than the slot can crush the media or keep the door from closing, and the furnace may not run.

Where to look

  • A slot on the furnace or air handler. A cover on the side, bottom, or top slides or lifts by hand. The filter is in that slot, not in the burner compartment.
  • A return grille. In a hallway, wall, or ceiling, the filter sits behind a grille that opens by hand. That’s the filter for some systems. There may be one in the slot and one in the grille. Use only the location your owner’s manual names. Two filters stacked in one slot choke the air.
  • A manufactured-home furnace. Many pull room air through louvers on the closet door, and some hold the filter on that door. Don’t block the louvers with stored items. If the only filter is behind a panel that needs tools, or the panel opens onto the burners, stop and call.
  • If you can’t find a slot without opening a burner door, stop. Don’t remove furnace doors to hunt for it.

How to change it

  1. Set the thermostat to Off and wait until the blower stops. That does not disconnect power.
  2. Open only the filter cover. Note the arrow on the old filter. The new arrow points the same way, toward the furnace or blower.
  3. Put in one filter. The printed size should match the old frame. If the old filter has no printing, measure that frame. Don’t measure the empty slot by sticking a tape into the blower.
  4. Close the cover so it sits flat. A door that isn’t seated can stop the furnace.
  5. Put the thermostat back to Heat or Cool and the fan to Auto.

How often

ENERGY STAR says to check the filter every month in heavy-use seasons. If it looks dirty after a month, change it. At a minimum, change it every 3 months. Pets, smoke, and a renovation clog a filter sooner. Your owner’s manual wins if it gives a different interval. A washable filter is cleaned only the way that manual describes, then dried before it goes back. Don’t run the system with the slot empty.

A filter that is the right size and still filthy can cause short cycling, weak air, or a higher bill. It will not fix a furnace that never starts. Use furnace not turning on for that.
