Smart Thermostat Has No Power or Won’t Connect

Last updated October 29, 2026.

If you smell burning at the thermostat or the furnace, see sparks, or a carbon monoxide alarm is sounding, stop. Burning or sparks: get away and call 911 if there is smoke or fire. A CO alarm: get everyone outside and call 911. If you smell gas or rotten eggs in the air, not just from the hot water, leave the house now and call the gas utility or 911 from outside. Don’t touch light switches or phones, or use lighters, matches, or anything that could spark or flame while you’re inside.

Blank screen

  1. If your model takes batteries and the manual shows a cover that opens by hand, put in fresh batteries of the type it names. If the cover doesn’t match, stop.
  2. Don’t pull the thermostat off the wall to look for wires. A C wire, a power adapter, or a kit inside the furnace means opening equipment. That’s a stop. Call the installer or a licensed HVAC professional.
  3. Look at the furnace or air-handler blower door. Some systems stop when that door isn’t seated. Don’t take the door off. If it is hanging open and you can push it shut without tools and without reaching inside, push it shut. If you’d have to remove it, leave it and call.
  4. Look at the furnace switch. Don’t flip it. If it is off, leave it and call. People turn that switch off on purpose after a leak or a smell.
  5. Furnace breakers are never reset. Look at the breaker marked for the furnace. If the handle is in the middle or off, leave it and call a licensed HVAC professional. Never open the breaker panel cover or a disconnect box.
  6. If lights and other equipment in the house are out too, the thermostat isn’t the only thing without power. Check with the utility before you take the thermostat apart.

Wi-Fi or the app won’t connect

  1. Restart the router and wait until it is back online.
  2. If your thermostat needs a 2.4 GHz network, check that one is on. Many phones use 5 GHz and the thermostat cannot join that band.
  3. Reconnect the thermostat to Wi-Fi using the maker’s steps on the screen you already see. If the screen doesn’t match, stop. Don’t keep pressing through menus.
  4. If it shows a Wi-Fi hardware error, or it still fails after a router restart, stop and call. Don’t open the thermostat.

No heat after a new install

Look at the thermostat you already use. Don’t take it off the wall. Set mode to Heat, fan to Auto, and the setpoint a few degrees above the room temperature. Fan On blows room-temperature air between heating cycles, which can feel cold. Some thermostats wait a few minutes before they start. Wait a few minutes. A new thermostat can sit on a delay. If a screen asks you to pick equipment type, wiring, or an installer setting, or it asks for a password, stop and call the installer. Don’t guess. The furnace may be fine and the setup may be wrong.

If the screen looks normal and the furnace still doesn’t start, continue with furnace not turning on. Don’t change the furnace’s wiring to match a diagram from the internet.
