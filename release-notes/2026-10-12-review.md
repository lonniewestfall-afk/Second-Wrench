## Rules applied during reconciliation

The preserved handoff doctrine, `legal/proper-notice-pattern.md`, cross-lane rulings R1–R18, D-026 audit strings, and the operator technical decisions in `audit/hvac-signoff-2026-10-11.md` are the starting record. References live in the read-only handoff archive, outside this public bundle. D-026 takes precedence over conflicting earlier lane drafts; stricter technical limits remain in effect.

- Hazard and professional exits must be reachable without an acknowledgement. New hazards override ordinary troubleshooting, waits, and continuation.
- No refrigerant handling, gas work, electrical/combustion compartment access, safety bypass, live tests, ladder access, reaching through a grille, or touching coil fins.
- Owner steps require the actual model/access to match and the appropriate notice. A thermostat/remote Off request is not electrical isolation. Owner-manual instructions that require a prohibited step do not become eligible merely by linking to the manual.
- A first trip, repeat trip, unknown/intentional shutdown, or furnace breaker concern is referred. Eligible seasonal AC restoration and known accidental furnace-switch restoration are distinct from clearing a fault.
- People needing heat take priority over experiments or equipment reassurance. The 18 °C / approximately 64 °F indoor threshold is a triage policy informed by WHO; it is not a universal boundary of protection. Vulnerability, falling temperature, and uncertainty also matter.
- Service notes describe reports and unknowns, not asserted repairs or component diagnoses. Typed optional details are not silently attached to feedback/activity reporting.
- Public copy must not claim the work was professionally or legally approved. `advancedRepairsEnabled`, `aiThermostatEnabled`, `mirrorsEnabled`, and `stageLatchStepsEnabled` remain false. Review flags remain false where verification has not occurred.
